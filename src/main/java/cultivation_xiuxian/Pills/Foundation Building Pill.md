4. Foundation Building Pill (Zhu Ji Dan)
Purpose: Forces diffuse gaseous Qi to condense into a permanent Dantian core.

Recipe & Ingredients
1 Mature Dragon-Blood Fruit (Core - Emperor): Extremely rare; contains raw origin vitality.

1 Heaven-Earth Spiritual Flower (Core - Emperor): Provides a massive, condensed Qi capacity.

2 Stalks of Violet Sun Grass (Assistant - Minister): Creates internal heat needed to melt and compress gaseous Qi.

1 Stalk of 500-Year Snow Lotus (Assistant - Envoy): Neutralizes excess heat to prevent "Fire Deviation" (spontaneous combustion).

1 Stage-2 Beast Core + 3 Drops of High-Grade Spirit Liquid (Catalyst/Binder): Forces the final solidification phase.

Refining Procedure
Set Dual Flame: Requires alternating between Extreme Dragon Fire and Frigid Ice Fire.

Liquefy Core Fruits: Melt the Dragon-Blood Fruit and Heaven-Earth Spiritual Flower under Extreme Dragon Fire until a thick, golden-red liquid forms.

Balance Elements: Add the Violet Sun Grass to raise the energy density, followed immediately by the 500-Year Snow Lotus using Frigid Ice Fire to prevent the cauldron from exploding.

Inject Catalyst: Pour in the High-Grade Spirit Liquid and drop the Stage-2 Beast Core into the golden-red mixture.

Grand Condensation: Rotate the furnace rapidly while applying maximum spiritual pressure to force the liquid into a spherical shape. Once stable, extinguish the fire completely. Yields 1 golden, high-tier Foundation Building Pill.
