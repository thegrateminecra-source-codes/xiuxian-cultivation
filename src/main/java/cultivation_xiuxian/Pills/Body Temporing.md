1. Body Tempering Pill (Cui Ti Dan)
Purpose: Hardens bones, skin, and tendons to endure internal Qi pressure.

Recipe & Ingredients
1 Root of Iron-Bark Root (Core - Emperor): Matured for at least 30 years to maximize physical density.

3 Stalks of Blood-Nourishing Flower (Assistant - Minister): Prevents internal hemorrhaging during bone density transformation.

1 Essence-Infused Bone-Refining Fruit (Assistant - Envoy): Softens bone matrix to allow restructuring.

3 Drops of Rank-1 Tiger-Beast Blood (Catalyst/Binder): Provides violent physical energy to force bodily adaptation.

Refining Procedure
Ignite the Furnace: Bring the alchemy furnace to a High Earth Fire temperature.

Refine the Core: Toss in the Iron-Bark Root first. Burn off the bark until only the golden, liquid core essence remains.

Add Assistants: Lower the heat to a medium simmer. Introduce the Blood-Nourishing Flowers and Bone-Refining Fruit simultaneously to merge their cooling liquids into the Iron-Bark essence.

Catalyze & Fuse: Drip the Rank-1 Tiger-Beast Blood directly into the center of the liquid. The mixture will react violently—immediately seal the furnace lid to trap the volatile energy.

Form Pill: Maintain steady pressure for 2 hours until the medicinal liquid condenses into 3 solid, deep-red pills.
