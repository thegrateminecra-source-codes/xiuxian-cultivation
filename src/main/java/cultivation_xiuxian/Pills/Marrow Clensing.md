2. Marrow Cleansing Pill (Xi Sui Dan)
Purpose: Flushes out turbid mortal impurities through the pores.

Recipe & Ingredients
1 Blossom of Cleansing Jade Lotus (Core - Emperor): Must be harvested from clean water sources.

2 Stalks of Nine-Leaf Impurity-Purifying Herb (Assistant - Minister): Draws out trapped toxins from deep within organs.

1 Stem of Clear Heart Grass (Assistant - Envoy): Calms the cultivator’s mind during painful impurity extraction.

1 Shed Snake Skin (Assistant): Facilitates the shedding of dead skin/clogged pores.

1 Vial of High-Purity Spirit Spring Water (Catalyst/Binder): Dilutes harsh medicinal properties so internal organs are not damaged.
Refining Procedure
Stabilize Furnace: Maintain a gentle, Low Water-Tempted Fire. High heat will destroy the delicate purifying properties of the lotus.

Extract Lotus Essence: Dissolve the Cleansing Jade Lotus into the Spirit Spring Water inside the cauldrons until it forms a translucent blue gel.

Melt Herbs: Add the Nine-Leaf Impurity-Purifying Herb, Clear Heart Grass, and Shed Snake Skin one by one, allowing each to fully dissolve into the gel base.

Purify & Condense: Slowly raise the temperature to boil off excess water vapor while retaining the active medicinal properties.

Form Pill: Gather the remaining paste using spiritual sense and mold it into 2 pale-blue pills.
