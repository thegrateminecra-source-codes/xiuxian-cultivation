3. Qi Gathering Pill (Ju Qi Dan)
Purpose: Floods the lower abdomen with external energy to ignite Dantian formation.

Recipe & Ingredients
1 Root of 100-Year Spirit Ginseng (Core - Emperor): Rich in ambient natural spiritual energy.

2 Stalks of Spirit-Gathering Grass (Core - Emperor): Acts as an antenna to draw in surrounding Qi during refining.

1 Aged Lingzhi Mushroom (Assistant - Minister): Converts raw environmental Qi into a form human meridians can accept.

4 Leaves of Meridian-Soothing Fern (Assistant - Envoy): Coats the inner walls of meridians to prevent ruptures.

1 Low-Grade Spirit Beast Core (Catalyst/Binder): Acts as an ignition spark.

Refining Procedure
Preheat Cauldron: Bring the furnace to a Medium Fire.

Pulverize Core Ingredients: Grind the 100-Year Spirit Ginseng and Spirit-Gathering Grass into powder. Heat until they emit a dense white mist inside the furnace.

Add Protective Layer: Add the Meridian-Soothing Fern and Lingzhi Mushroom to combine with the mist, creating a protective, viscous liquid.

Ignite Core: Drop the Spirit Beast Core into the furnace. The core will shatter, releasing a sudden surge of compressed Qi.

Compress & Pill Form: Quickly manipulate the furnace pressure to force the expanding white mist into the liquid base. Cool the furnace rapidly to freeze the energy into 5 glossy white pills.
